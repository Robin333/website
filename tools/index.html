Tools

Many episodes end with a named tool: a prompt, a question or a test you can use at work. They're collected here, ready to copy.

The story-or-evidence check

Here's a check worth borrowing, for any verdict, from an AI or anyone else.

Ask: would this evidence look any different if the story were false?

Femi's warmth wouldn't have. Neither would the red spine of a ledger nobody had seen.

What they found much harder was knowing when their evidence meant nothing.

What gets judged now

If the polish tells you nothing, what do people go on?

What's in the deck: the strategy, the results, the proposal.
The person presenting it: do I trust this person, or not?
And the moment after the last slide, when someone asks a question the deck doesn't answer.

When every deck looks good, a good-looking deck tells you nothing. Polish was a costly signal, and a signal like that only works while it's hard to fake.

The fed-or-found map

If you drew this map for your own job, what would it show? Which of your work is fed, and which is found? Which relationships are thickening, and which are thinning? And who drew the lines?

Found work: you decide what's worth doing, and there's always more. Fed work: it arrives in a queue, and how much there is isn't up to you. Fed doesn't mean lesser, and most jobs are a mix, so ask which work is fed, not who, and what the time freed from it could become.

The hen-house check

Review this for clarity of meaning. List the questions my writing leaves unanswered, most important first, and no more than three. Quote the words that leave each one unclear. Ask me those questions before you rewrite anything, and don't invent missing facts.

To keep it, save it as a standing instruction with a trigger ("When I say 'hen-house check', review my text like this: …"). ChatGPT: Settings → Personalization → Custom instructions. Claude: Settings → Your Claude → Instructions. Microsoft 365 Copilot (work accounts): Settings → Personalization → Custom instructions, a preview feature your admin may have switched off.
