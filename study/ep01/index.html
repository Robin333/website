Episode 1: the study

The data, code and pre-registered designs behind We Tested Whether AI Still Writes in Clichés. Every figure in the video comes from these files.

In brief

  • Main study (pre-registered): 7 models from 3 vendors, 24 business-writing tasks, 5,880 outputs. Drafting with a vague or specific brief, plainly or told to avoid clichés; and rewriting a sentence "more specific", "clearer" or with the hen-house check.
  • Extension (pre-registered): the same protocol on three OpenAI models, 2,520 outputs.
  • Follow-up (exploratory, frozen before the run): three OpenAI models across five genres, 270 outputs.
  • Human check: the author blind-coded 60 replies, with the model and instruction hidden.

"Delve", in any form, appears 0 times in the 8,400 outputs from 10 models. That includes the 6,000 with no instruction to avoid clichés. It is also 0 in the 270 follow-up pieces.

The files

  • README: what's here, how to re-run the analysis, and the limitations in plain words
  • Designs: main study, extension, follow-up; deviations log
  • Inputs: the 24 tasks, the stock-phrase lexicon
  • Outputs: main study (9 MB), extension, follow-up; judge codes; blind-coding key and comparison
  • Results: main summary, extension summary, follow-up summary, per-output measures, every figure in the video
  • Code: run.py, analyse.py, judge.py, script_numbers.py, and the model-access stand-in
