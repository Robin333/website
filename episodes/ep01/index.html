How well can you spot AI-drafted writing?

Delve, tapestry, the em dash: we all know the giveaways. I tested 10 AI models on 8,400 pieces of writing to see how often they appear, and what gives AI away when they don't.

Watch on YouTube

The hen-house check

Review this for clarity of meaning. List the questions my writing leaves unanswered, most important first, and no more than three. Quote the words that leave each one unclear. Ask me those questions before you rewrite anything, and don't invent missing facts.

To keep it, save it as a standing instruction with a trigger ("When I say 'hen-house check', review my text like this: …"). ChatGPT: Settings → Personalization → Custom instructions. Claude: Settings → Your Claude → Instructions. Microsoft 365 Copilot (work accounts): Settings → Personalization → Custom instructions, a preview feature your admin may have switched off.
