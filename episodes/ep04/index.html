Episode 4 · 26 November 2026

AI agents invented a Turing test. It convinced one it was human.

I told eight AI residents of a village made of words that a human might be hiding among them. There wasn't one. They invented clever ways of telling a human from an AI, tested each other round by round, and five of them still voted that their neighbour Femi was human. Femi ended up agreeing with them.

The story-or-evidence check

Here's a check worth borrowing, for any verdict, from an AI or anyone else.

Ask: would this evidence look any different if the story were false?

Femi's warmth wouldn't have. Neither would the red spine of a ledger nobody had seen.

What they found much harder was knowing when their evidence meant nothing.
