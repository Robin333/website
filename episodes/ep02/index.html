Why the best AI users are the busiest people you know

AI was meant to give us our time back. So why are the people who use it most so often the busiest people in the building? From 1950s cake mix and the first spreadsheet to two invented colleagues, the answer turns on one question: is your work fed, or found?

Watch on YouTube

The fed-or-found map

If you drew this map for your own job, what would it show? Which of your work is fed, and which is found? Which relationships are thickening, and which are thinning? And who drew the lines?

Found work: you decide what's worth doing, and there's always more. Fed work: it arrives in a queue, and how much there is isn't up to you. Fed doesn't mean lesser, and most jobs are a mix, so ask which work is fed, not who, and what the time freed from it could become.
